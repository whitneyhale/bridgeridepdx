## Riding it

Paths 14 ft wide on both sides, shared with people walking. Map data shows the south path for eastbound riders and the north path for westbound. MAX, buses, and the streetcar run in the middle. No private cars or trucks.

## Getting on and off

West end: South Waterfront, by the OHSU/South Waterfront MAX station and S Bond Avenue. The Willamette Greenway Trail runs along the river here.

East end: the Central Eastside, near OMSI and the OMSI MAX station, just north of SE Caruthers. The Eastbank Esplanade is close.

## Good to know

Watch for rails where the paths meet the streets at each end. If one path is closed for work, both directions share the other one.

## History

Opened in 2015 for the MAX Orange Line. "Tilikum" is a Chinook Jargon word meaning people, tribe, or family. Before it was named, people called it the Portland-Milwaukie Light Rail Bridge or Caruthers Crossing.
