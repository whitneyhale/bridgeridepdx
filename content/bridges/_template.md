## Riding it

Which side to ride in each direction, what the bike facility is, surface, grade, anything a rider feels.

## Getting on and off

Entrances at each end, ramps or stairs, the turn to make, what to watch for.

## Good to know

Lifts, closures, crowding, lighting, noise, views.

## History

Two or three sentences. Year, what it replaced, why it matters.
