## Riding it

24 ft wide and 475 ft long, for bikes and people walking only. It crosses I-84 and the Union Pacific tracks on a tied-arch span. It is a good overpass, so the app prefers it over other ways across I-84.

## Getting on and off

South end: NE 7th Avenue in the Central Eastside Industrial District.

North end: NE 7th Avenue in the Lloyd District.

## Good to know

Part of the Green Loop, the planned car-free loop around the central city.

## History

Opened July 31, 2022. Named for Congressman Earl Blumenauer.
