## Riding it

Bikes use the wide sidewalks on both sides, about 10 ft each. Going east, use the south side. Going west, use the north side. Expect a lot of other riders and people walking at commute times.

## Getting on and off

West end: SW Naito Parkway and 1st Avenue, joining SW Madison and Main. The Willamette Greenway Trail along Waterfront Park is right there.

East end: SE Hawthorne Boulevard and SE Madison, near Grand Avenue and MLK. The paths connect down to the Eastbank Esplanade.

## Good to know

It is a lift bridge, so a river lift can stop you while the span is raised. Multnomah County sometimes closes one side for repairs. When that happens the open side may carry both directions. Check closures before a timed ride.

## History

Opened in 1910. It is Portland's oldest highway bridge. The sidewalks were widened to 10 ft in 1998 and 1999, and joined to the Eastbank Esplanade in 2001. Painted green with red trim since that renovation.
