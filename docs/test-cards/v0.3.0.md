Release: v0.3.0   Task: ride basics (1.4, 1.5, 1.6) and all screens   Replaces the 0.2.2 test card

What changed: the app can follow a line. Pick a built-in ride (Hawthorne out, Steel back; Sellwood loop) or one of your own recorded rides in Plan, tap Go, and the ride screen shows a bike marker with a heading pointer, the next bridge with its direction and side, and miles left. If GPS drops, the marker turns to "estimated", keeps moving along the line for 10 s, then holds; it does not jump backward. More than 40 m from the line for 8 s shows one "off the line" warning with the way back; it clears when you rejoin. There is no rerouting and no turn-by-turn yet. New screens: Plan, Ride, Ride summary (bridges crossed, your rating), Bridge passport (Library), Bridge sheet (tap a bridge dot). Rain and gloves: ride buttons are 64 dp tall, End needs a 1 s hold, Lock makes the screen ignore touches until you hold Unlock. Installs over 0.2.2 and keeps your rides. Install `bridgeride-v0.3.0.apk`; the `-debug` file is only for pulling logs.

Parts drawn but labeled "Not built yet" in the app: adding or reordering stops, a route to an address or bridge, themed rides, street-choice questions, Home and Work, route style.

Tested before release (by Claude): 97 unit tests (follow a line, off-route rule, GPS gaps with replayed rides, heading and compass, bridge crossing detection, both built-in rides ridden start to finish with GPS noise), lint, data checks, and five Maestro flows on GitHub's emulator against the release build. Flow 04 rides the Hawthorne westbound with simulated GPS, takes a wrong turn, comes back, locks and unlocks, ends, and checks the crossing in the summary, the passport and the bridge sheet. Flow 05 does the Steel eastbound in the dark look. Screenshots of every screen in both looks are attached to the CI run.

Not tested by Claude, because the emulator cannot: a real GPS gap under the Esplanade decks, the compass, sunlight, wet fingers, gloves, the marker's motion on a real ride. These are your tests.

Where the built-in rides start: the Eastbank Esplanade, about 100 m south of the Steel Bridge. Start anywhere; the banner shows how far the line is and which way.

Desk test (5 min):
1. Map: tap a bridge dot. The sheet shows the bridge, whether bikes may cross, and the side to ride.
2. Plan a ride, Hawthorne out, Steel back. The line is drawn; the stops list both bridges with direction and side.
3. Library, Passport: 12 bikeable bridges, none stamped yet (unless an earlier recorded ride crossed one).
4. Library, Rides: your earlier rides are there. Details opens the summary of one.

Bike test (45 min): ride Hawthorne out, Steel back from the Esplanade.
1. Tap Go at home or at the start. Check the banner before you reach the line ("to the line", a distance, a direction).
2. On the Esplanade: watch the marker under the Burnside and Morrison decks and on the floating walkway.
3. Hawthorne westbound: the banner counts down to the bridge, then says "On it".
4. In Waterfront Park, take one wrong turn on purpose. Ride a block away, wait for the warning, ride back.
5. Tap Lock. Tap and swipe the screen. Hold Unlock.
6. Steel lower deck eastbound, back to the start. Hold End.
7. Summary: both bridges listed with the right direction. Rate the route. Export the GPX.
8. Lock the phone for the last 10 minutes of any ride (the open 0.1 test), then check the summary's GPS line.

Rain and gloves (at home is fine): with a wet finger or a glove, tap Lock, hold Unlock, hold End.

Pass when: the marker stays on the line and points the way you ride; it stays calm under the decks (estimated, no jumping); the wrong turn warns once and clears itself; both crossings appear in the summary and the passport with the right direction; Lock holds; no GPS gap over 10 s while the screen was on.

Questions:
1. Under the decks: what did the marker do, and for how long was it "estimated"?
2. Stopped at a light: did the pointer face the way you were facing?
3. The wrong turn: was the warning too early, too late, or about right? Was 8 s the right wait?
4. Map turning with the line: right, or would you rather north stay up?
5. Banner and numbers: readable at a glance while riding?
6. Lock and End with wet fingers or gloves: any misses?
7. Bridge sheet: is any side-of-bridge text wrong? Sellwood, Interstate, Glenn Jackson and Blumenauer say "side not confirmed" on purpose; tell me the side if you know it.

Send back: pass or fail per step, the GPX, answers, a screenshot if something looks wrong.

If a step fails: Claude fixes it in v0.3.1 and you retest only that step. Your GPX becomes a replay test. The offline map and on-phone routing (0.4) are built next; nothing after 0.4 is built until this card is answered.
