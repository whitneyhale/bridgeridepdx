Release: v0.2.2   Task: theme and release build   Replaces the 0.1, 0.2 and 0.2.1 test cards

What changed: the app has its two looks. Light is E (Portland Explorer), dark is C (Modern Urban Cyclist). Settings has a Look switch: Auto, Light, Dark. The map is restyled to match and bridge ends are mustard dots (light) or white dots (dark), never the route color. This is the first shrunk release build, which should start and scroll faster. Recording works as before. Installs over 0.1 or 0.2 and keeps your rides. Install `bridgeride-v0.2.2.apk`; the `-debug` file is only for pulling logs.

Tested before release (by Claude, on GitHub's emulator): unit tests, lint, palette check for both looks, three Maestro flows on the release build (record a ride, map and tabs, switch looks and restart). Screenshots of both looks are attached to the CI run. v0.2.1 failed Claude's screenshot review (ride numbers ran together, Settings could not scroll, GPS-off banner used the good-state color), so it was replaced by v0.2.2 before you tested it.

Desk test (5 min):
1. Open the app. Cream map, mustard dots at both ends of each bridge.
2. Settings, Look, Dark. Back to Map: dark map, white dots.
3. Close and reopen the app. It is still dark. Set Look to Auto.
4. Library: your earlier rides are still there.
5. Take the phone outside. Read the map and the bottom card in both looks.

Bike test (the open 0.1 test, 30 min): ride a loop over one or two bridges. Screen on for 20 minutes, then locked for 10. Stop, open Library, export the GPX.

Speed check (phone plugged into the PC, optional): tell Claude the phone is plugged in; it measures start time, memory and frame times.

Pass when: both looks are readable outside, the Look choice survives a restart, earlier rides survived the update, and the GPX track follows the streets you rode with no gap over 10 s while the screen was on.

Questions:
1. Which look did you leave it on to ride, and could you read it in sun?
2. Anything too small, too faint or too busy on the map?
3. Gaps while locked: what did the bottom card show (Gaps >10 s, Longest gap)?

Send back: pass or fail per test, the GPX, answers, a screenshot if something looks wrong.

If a step fails: Claude fixes it in v0.2.3 and you retest only that step. Ride basics (0.3) are built next either way; nothing after 0.3 is built until this card is answered.
