Hawthorne Bridge

Oregon's busiest bike and transit bridge. Ride the south side going east, the north side going west.

Where it is

Willamette bridge 7 of 11, north to south. Morrison is 0.3 river miles north. Marquam is 0.4 south (no bikes), then Tilikum.

Getting on and off

West end: SW Naito Parkway and 1st Avenue, joining SW Madison and Main. The Willamette Greenway Trail is right there.

East end: SE Hawthorne Boulevard and SE Madison, near Grand and MLK. The paths connect down to the Eastbank Esplanade.

Good to know

Lift bridge: a river lift can stop you. The county sometimes closes one side; the open side may carry both directions.
