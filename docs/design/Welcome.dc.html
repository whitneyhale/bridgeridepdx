Bridge Ride PDX

Plan bike rides across Portland's bridges on greenways and car-free paths. Free.

  • 1Say a bridge any way you like: "the Hawthorne", "red bridge", "Tillicum".
  • 2Pick the direction. The app takes you onto the right side of the bridge.
  • 3Save it, ride it, and keep a log of every ride.

Before your first ride on a Samsung

Set battery use for this app to not optimized, keep it off the sleeping apps list, and start the ride before you lock the screen. Location is asked for when you press Start ride.
